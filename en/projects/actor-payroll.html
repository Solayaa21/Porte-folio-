Dubbing studio, Montreal · 2025 to 2026

Actor payroll and contracts

An Excel tool that turns the recording session schedule into fees, taxes, contracts and union remittances ready to send. Rebuilt in 2026 to be simpler and more reliable.

The need

Every recording session leads to a fee calculated under the union agreement, taxes, a contract and a remittance. Done by hand, it was slow and error prone.

What I built

An import of the session schedule, automatic calculation of fees and taxes by union and project, a status on each line that says what is missing, and documents generated in one click.

What it changes

The admin team follows every session through to payment with no re-entry, and immediately sees what blocks a document: a missing social insurance number or union number.

The workflow, step by step
  1. Schedule importSessions come in from the studio schedule. The latest import log shows what was added or changed.
  2. CalculationFees, subtotals and taxes by union, role and project rates.
  3. ChecksEach line gets a status: ready, or what is still to complete.
  4. DocumentsContracts, union remittances and timesheets generated for the ready lines.
Screens
Technical details
  • Three linked databases: sessions, projects with their rates, and voice actors with their payment details.
  • An alias table reconciles names written differently in the schedule.
  • Excel 365 formulas (LET, XLOOKUP) for the calculations, and VBA for the import and document generation.
  • Visible business rules: an actor's first visit, special rate, forced amount, each in its own column.
  • Rebuilt in 2026 from a first version: fewer tabs, an import that explains its changes, readable statuses.
Tools
  • Excel 365
  • VBA
  • Structured tables
  • Conditional formatting
