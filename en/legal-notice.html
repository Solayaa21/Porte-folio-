Personal data

Fonts are hosted with the site. Without your consent, no cookie is set and no data is sent to any measurement service.

Contact form. The details you enter (name, e-mail, company, phone, message) are sent through the host Netlify. They are only used to reply to your request and are kept for at most three years after our last exchange.

Advertising cookies. With your consent, the site loads the Google Ads tag (Google Ireland Limited) to measure ad performance: it sets cookies and tells Google that a request was sent after an ad click. Your choice is kept for six months on your device.

The host records technical logs, including IP addresses, needed to run and secure the service.

If you contact me by e-mail or phone, your details are only used to reply to you. You can ask to access, correct or delete them at contact@alexandremugular.fr, and lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority.
